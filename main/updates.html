The definitive v3.0.0 of Sonic Jump Revival is now released on Sonic Mobirev and the SAGE EXPO 2026!

Additionally, the Mobage version of Sonic Advance's Android port has been archived on Sonic Mobirev!

Welcome to the Sonic Mobirev website! The website is now online and open for anyone to see!

Sonic Mobirev is a fan-made project dedicated to the Sonic mobile games where you can:

  • Download Sonic abandonwares and mods for Android devices.
  • Learn more about the history of the different mobile services and their games.
  • Download content from the different Sonic mobile games (application icons, sprite sheets, videos, and more...)
