Upload Date: 09/11/2026


Version 3.0.0 release:
The definitive v3.0.0 of Sonic Jump Revival is now released on Sonic Mobirev and the SAGE EXPO 2026!

This major update gives new features and quality of life improvements! The new features include a new playable character (Amy Rose), 33.3fps (with an option to toggle between 16.7fps and 33.3fps), multi-screen orientation, gamepad and keyboard support.


Upload Date: 04/22/2026


Version 3.0.0 (Beta) release:
Sonic Jump Anniversary has turned into Sonic Jump Revival!

The beta version of Sonic Jump Revival has been released alongside the Sonic Mobirev project to be part of the Sonic Mobirev series of Sonic Android apps.

This update introduces new features such as the Ukrainian language made by Alex Khayrullin, the original J2ME fonts, and Tails & Knuckles are now playable!

However, being a beta version, there are still updates on the way. This version isn't the definitive version.

Happy Halloween! A new update has been released at this special day of the year!

Upload Date: 10/04/2025


Sonic Jump Anniversary is now released!

Sonic Jump Anniversary is a mod of the Android port of the original Sonic Jump to celebrate the 20th anniversary of Sonic Jump.
This mod makes this version of Sonic Jump playable on modern Android devices. But it also features a soundtrack select, new game modes, a multi-language feautre, and more!
