Upload Date: 09/11/2026

Version 3.0.0 release:
The definitive v3.0.0 of Sonic Jump Revival is now released on Sonic Mobirev and the SAGE EXPO 2026!

This major update gives new features and quality of life improvements! The new features include a new playable character (Amy Rose), 33.3fps (with an option to toggle between 16.7fps and 33.3fps), multi-screen orientation, gamepad and keyboard support.
